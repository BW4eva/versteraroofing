About Us

Too many roofing companies chase quick profits instead of quality. That’s not us. At Verstera Roofing, we’re on a mission to build strong, storm-resistant roofs that stand up to Texas hail and protect both your home and the environment. We started in Harker Heights, and now have opened branches in Austin and San Antonio.

We use premium materials, focus on honest work, and treat every roof like it’s our own. Because for us, this isn’t just a business — it’s a way to give back to the place we call home.

Proudly veteran-owned. Locally operated. Built to last for Central Texas.
